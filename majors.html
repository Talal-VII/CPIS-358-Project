Majors

Explore the majors and read all about them!

Computer Science (CS)

Computer Science is all about how computers and software really work. It has a lot of math and logic.

Learn More

Information Technology (IT)

IT is about the technology that companies use every day, like networks, servers and security. Instead of building new software, you make sure everything runs well and stays safe. It is more hands-on and has less theory than CS.

Learn More

Information Systems (IS)

Information Systems mixes technology with business. You learn how companies use systems and data to work better and make decisions. You still code, but you also learn how to understand what a business needs.
