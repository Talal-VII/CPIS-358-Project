About Us

Welcome to MajorMate! Where you can find all the information you need about different majors.

Why we made MajorMate

Just like you, we also went through the struggle of trying to find the right major for ourselves. We always just asked people, and never had the full information, or just went with what our parents told us. That's why we created MajorMate, so you get all the information you need to help you make the best decision, from breif to detailed information about each major to what courses they use and in what university they are offered.
